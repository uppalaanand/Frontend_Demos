Banglure

Bangalore, officially known as Bengaluru, is the capital city of the Indian state of Karnataka. It is located in the southern part of India and is known as the "Silicon Valley of India" due to its thriving IT industry. With a population of over 12 million people, Bangalore is the third most populous city in India. The city is also known for its pleasant climate, vibrant culture, and numerous parks and gardens, including Lalbagh Botanical Garden and Cubbon Park.
