Delhi

Delhi, officially the National Capital Territory of Delhi (NCT), is a city and a union territory of India containing New Delhi, the capital of India. It is bordered by Haryana on three sides and by Uttar Pradesh to the east. The NCT covers an area of 1,484 square kilometres (573 sq mi). According to the 2011 census, it had a population of 16.8 million, making it the second most populous city in India after Mumbai and the most populous union territory. The city is also the second most populous urban agglomeration in India after Mumbai and the largest urban agglomeration in northern India.
