Hyderabad

Hyderabad is the capital of the Indian state of Telangana. It is also the de jure capital of Andhra Pradesh, serving as the common capital of both states until 2024. Hyderabad is located on the banks of the Musi River in the Deccan Plateau region of South India. With a population of 10 million, it is the fourth most populous city and sixth most populous urban agglomeration in India. Hyderabad is known for its rich history, culture, and architecture, including landmarks such as the Charminar, Golconda Fort, and Qutb Shahi Tombs.
